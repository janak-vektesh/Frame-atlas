Practical first

Miniatures and mattes

Before CG, painted glass and scale models extended sets; Star Wars (1977) pushed motion-control miniatures forward.

Motion capture

Actors’ movements are recorded and applied to digital characters, as with Gollum in The Lord of the Rings.

LED volumes

Screens track the camera so backgrounds shift in correct perspective, giving actors real light and context.

Pixels meet real light

Visual effects start long before post-production. Supervisors join early to plan which shots need blue screens, which need real sets, and which can be solved with clever camera angles.

Virtual production changes the order of work. Environments are built first, the camera moves inside them on set, and many decisions once made months later are made in the room.
