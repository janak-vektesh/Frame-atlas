Four firsts

First public screening

The Lumière brothers held the first paid public film screening in Paris on 28 December 1895.

Sound arrives

The Jazz Singer (1927) made synchronised sound a business, and 24 frames a second became the standard.

One shot, 96 minutes

Russian Ark (2002) is a single unbroken Steadicam shot through the Hermitage, running about 96 minutes.

The Wilhelm scream

A stock scream first heard in Distant Drums (1951) has since turned up in countless films as an in-joke.

The archive

A short history

Film history moves in jumps: a new machine arrives, audiences learn a new habit, and storytellers invent a grammar to match it.

Many early rules were accidents of technology. Even 24 frames per second came from sound and cost, not from any perfect number.
