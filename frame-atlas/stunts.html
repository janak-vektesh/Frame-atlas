Four ways to survive

Keaton’s falling house

In Steamboat Bill, Jr. (1928), a heavy house front fell around Buster Keaton, who stood on a marked spot with an open window just inches either side.

Real vehicles, real impact

Mad Max: Fury Road (2015) built working vehicles and filmed most of its chases in camera, using digital effects mainly to enhance them.

The cliff jump

For Mission: Impossible – Dead Reckoning, Tom Cruise reportedly practised thousands of motorcycle jumps and hundreds of skydives first.

Rigs and safety

Teams plan with wires, air rams, ratchets, padded landings and safety crews, so the danger lives on screen and not on set.

Caught on camera

Risk, rehearsed

Every stunt begins on paper. The coordinator and director agree what the audience must see, then design the safest way to deliver it.

Rehearsal is slow on purpose. Speeds, distances, cameras and medics are locked before the first take, so spontaneity belongs to the performance, not the risk.
