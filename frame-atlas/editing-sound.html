Four ways to join

The match cut

2001: A Space Odyssey (1968) cuts from a thrown bone to an orbiting satellite, linking millions of years in one edit.

The hard cut

The default: an instant switch that, when done well, the audience never notices.

L-cut and J-cut

Sound from the next scene begins before the picture changes, or lingers after, making cuts feel smooth.

Sound design

Silence, room tone and one Foley detail can frighten more than music, because the audience fills in the rest.

The cutting room

Make the cut

In the edit room

Rhythm is the editor’s first tool. Short shots speed the pulse, long shots let a moment breathe, and a well-placed pause can feel louder than any explosion.

Sound carries half the experience. Dialogue, effects, music and silence are balanced in the final mix, often in a room built to sound like a cinema.
